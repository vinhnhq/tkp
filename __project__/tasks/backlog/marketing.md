## Ownership

Most of these tasks need the client's participation — they hold the customer relationships, the photos, the certifications, and the business knowledge. Dev team implements; client provides raw material.

Recommend assigning one person on the client side as the marketing point of contact before launch, so these tasks don't stall waiting for approval.
