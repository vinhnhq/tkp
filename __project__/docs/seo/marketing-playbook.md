# TKP Marketing & SEO Playbook

Reference doc. The "why" behind the tasks in [`backlog/marketing.md`](../../tasks/backlog/marketing.md). Written for non-specialists — no SEO jargon where plain language works.

---

## Mental model

Google ranks pages by **relevance + authority + trust**.

- **Relevance** = does this page answer the exact thing the user searched for?
- **Authority** = do other credible websites link to this one?
- **Trust** = is this a real, consistent, verifiable business?

Most amateurs obsess over relevance (keywords) and ignore authority and trust. For a local B2B manufacturer, **trust + authority do most of the work**, and TKP's 29 years in business is a moat competitors cannot fake.

---

## Effort distribution

- **20% technical SEO** (Sprint 03 Track A). Head tags, sitemap, JSON-LD, redirects.
- **80% real-world signals** (this playbook). Reviews, links, real content, real photos, real customers.

Technical is the foundation — without it nothing else works. But it is not enough on its own.

---

## What moves the needle for TKP specifically

Ranked by impact for a B2B carton manufacturer in HCMC:

1. **Google Business Profile + reviews.** For local queries, GBP outranks the website itself. Map pack = 3 listings. If TKP isn't there, nothing else matters.
2. **Backlinks from real websites.** Industry directories, supplier/customer "partners" pages, trade associations, press mentions. Biggest separator between rank #1 and rank #10.
3. **Content answering real sales questions.** Sit with sales for 2 hours, list every question a prospect has ever asked. That's the content plan. Honest specifics beat polished marketing fluff.
4. **Reviews everywhere** — Google, Facebook, Zalo. Buyers search "tkp carton review" before calling.
5. **Social proof on the site** — named testimonials, case studies with real numbers, real factory photos.
6. **Multilingual done right** — keywords researched per language, not translated. Chinese buyers search `瓦楞纸箱厂家`, not a literal translation of `thùng carton xuất khẩu`.
7. **Speed on mid-tier Android + 4G**, not iPhone + WiFi. VN reality check.
8. **Measurement that matters** — Search Console queries, lead conversion rate, attribution by channel. Not bounce rate, not DA scores.
9. **Patience** — 6-month game. Paid ads fill the gap during months 1-6 if leads are needed faster.
10. **NAP consistency** — Name / Address / Phone identical everywhere (website, GBP, Facebook, Zalo, directories). Inconsistency tanks local SEO.

## Things to explicitly NOT do

- **Scrape-and-reword blog content.** See [ADR-004](../decisions/ADR-004-original-content-over-scraped-blog.md).
- **Buy backlinks.** Google detects and penalizes. Penalties take months to recover.
- **Keyword stuff.** 2026 algorithms punish it.
- **Meta keywords tag.** Dead since 2009.
- **Translate without researching target-language keywords first.** You end up with pages nobody searches for.
- **Obsess over vanity metrics** (traffic, impressions, bounce rate) instead of lead conversion.

## Realistic timeline

| Month | What to expect |
|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Site fully indexed. Brand queries ("tkp carton") rank #1 with rich snippets. |
| 2-3 | Non-brand local queries moving to page 2-3. GBP calls increase. |
| 4-6 | Long-tail commercial queries hit page 1. Organic leads trickle in. |
| 6-12 | Compound growth kicks in — if effort stayed consistent. |

The biggest non-expert failure mode: expecting results in weeks, getting discouraged, abandoning strategy, chasing the next shiny trick. Don't.

## B2B-specific calibration

- Low search volume is fine. 50 searches/month where 5 are serious buyers beats 5000 searches/month with no buyers.
- One good export-grade customer pays for 12 months of SEO work. Calibrate effort accordingly.
- Don't need a blog post per week. 1 excellent pillar per month beats 4 mediocre ones.

## The 5-things-if-you-only-do-5

1. Google Business Profile + 10 real customer reviews.
2. Consistent NAP (Name / Address / Phone) across website, GBP, Facebook, Zalo, directories. Fix the email typo first (see `brief.md` §11).
3. 5 pillar blog posts in Vietnamese answering real sales questions, with specifics only TKP can cite.
4. 20 legitimate directory + partner-page listings linking to `tkpcarton.com`.
5. Search Console + GA4 installed, reviewed monthly, adjusted based on actual query data.
