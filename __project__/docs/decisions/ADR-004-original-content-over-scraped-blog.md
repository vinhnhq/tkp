# ADR-004 — Original Niche Content over Scraped-and-Reworded Blog

**Context:** There is a temptation to jump-start SEO by scraping existing articles about carton/packaging from the web, rewording them via LLM, and publishing them as TKP's blog. This looks like cheap content volume.

**Decision:** Do not scrape-and-reword. Write a smaller number (5–8) of original pillar posts grounded in TKP's 29 years of real operating experience — specific customer problems, specific materials, specific processes at the HCMC factory.

**Alternatives:**
- *Scrape-and-reword third-party articles*: rejected. Google's Helpful Content System and duplicate-content detection penalize reworded aggregations. TKP would compete against the original sources with zero topical authority and no distinctive angle. Short-term content volume, long-term ranking risk.
- *Pay a generic packaging-industry content farm*: rejected. Same problem — indistinguishable from every other manufacturer's blog. No experience-grounded specifics that let Google's quality signals recognize real expertise.
- *Skip blog entirely*: rejected. Long-tail commercial queries ("bao bì carton cho thanh long xuất Nhật", "flexo vs in phun chi phí") have real search demand and cannot be served by product pages alone.

**Consequences:**
- Content output is slower — 5–8 posts over a quarter rather than 50 posts in a week.
- Requires domain input from TKP's team (sales, production lead) that an external writer would not have. This is friction but also the moat.
- First-party photography and real job examples become part of the SEO asset, not just decoration.
- Expected outcome is lower post count but higher per-post ranking and conversion rate. Measured in GSC after 3 months — if pillars rank, expand the pattern; if not, revisit.
- EN/ZH translations of pillar posts only happen if the VN original performs. No speculative translation.
